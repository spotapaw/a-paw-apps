# Sample animal photographs

Demo content for Adopt-a-Paw. Every one is **CC0** from Wikimedia
Commons, which carries no attribution obligation — recorded here
anyway, because no obligation is not the same as no record.

These are real animals that are **not** for adoption. They stand in
for the sample rescues, which the app marks as Sample.
